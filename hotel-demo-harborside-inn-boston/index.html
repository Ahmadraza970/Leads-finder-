About Our Property

A Calm Haven on Boston waterfront

Located right opposite the New High Court in Kamta, Ismailganj, Harborside Inn provides an oasis of warm Awadhi hospitality and modern convenience.

Whether you are in town for legal hearings, corporate meetings, or a relaxing family holiday in Boston, our attentive staff, pristine rooms, in-house gourmet kitchen, and tranquil pool ensure your trip is smooth and pleasant.

📍 Prime Location

Directly on Boston waterfront with quick access to the Ring Road & Airport.

⏰ Flexible Timings

Check-in: 2:00 PM · Check-out: 12:00 PM · 24/7 Front Desk.

🍛 Culinary Excellence

Authentic North Indian, Awadhi kebabs and fresh morning spreads.

💼 Business & Family

High speed WiFi, work tables, and spacious interconnecting suites.
